Privacy and terms

Last updated November 15, 2026.

The NYC 15-Minute City Explorer is a free map that shows what you can walk to from a spot in New York. You can use it without an account. It has no ads and no analytics, and we don't sell or share your data.

When you search

  • The spot you pick (a click, an address, or "Use my location") is sent to our server to work out the walk. The server's request log, kept by our host Render for a limited time, records the spot's coordinates.
  • "Use my location" asks your browser for your location only when you press it, rounds it to about 10 meters, and uses it as the search spot. It isn't stored on our server.
  • The spot goes into the page's address (?lat=…&lng=…), so a link you share shows that spot. Think twice before sharing a link to your home.
  • To stop scripts from overloading the server, your IP address is counted for about a minute. We also keep daily totals of requests per page, with nothing about who made them.

Other services your browser talks to

  • Stadia Maps draws the base map. It sees your IP address and the part of the map you're looking at.
  • NYC Planning's GeoSearch suggests addresses. It sees your IP address and what you type in the address box.
  • Supabase handles sign-in. It sees your email, IP address, and sign-in times.
  • Render and Cloudflare host the site and see your IP address, like any web host.

If you create an account

  • Your email address and password. Supabase stores the password only in a scrambled (hashed) form.
  • Your saved places: the name you give each one and its location, up to 50.
  • Your needs: how much each kind of place matters to you, and whether you search walking or in a wheelchair.

We use these only to show them back to you. The only emails we send are the ones to confirm your address and reset your password.

You must be 13 or older to create an account.

Kept in your browser

Your last five spots, your needs, your walk or wheelchair choice, and, when you're signed in, a sign-in token. These stay on your device. The site sets no cookies of its own. Clear this site's data in your browser to remove them.

Deleting your account

Account > Delete account removes your saved places, your needs, and your sign-in (email and password) right away, and signs you out. It doesn't clear what's kept in your browser (see above), and copies in Render's and Supabase's logs and backups are deleted on their own schedules.
